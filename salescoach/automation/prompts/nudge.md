- names the specific commitment and when it came up, in the buyer's own words where possible ("On our 2 Sep call you mentioned you'd set up the CFO meeting");

- is three to six short sentences plus the sign-off;

- is finished text: no template brackets such as [Name], [date] or [your company]. Use the real names from the facts. The only bracket allowed anywhere is [SLOTS], and only in the body.
