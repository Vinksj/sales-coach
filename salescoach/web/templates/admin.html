People and teams

Who can sign in, what they are, and which managers read which team. Being an admin grants no access to anyone's calls or deals: an admin sees a team's work only when listed as one of its managers, like any manager.
Team setup: create a team, put reps in it, and name its managers. A manager then gets the Team page, reads everything the team records and comments on it; a manager can never change or send anything of a rep's (docs/manager.md).
